**Jobsite SoS model: starting with OA; scenario is a European highway/road construction site, driven by a regional requirement change.**

Modeling of the `heavy_equipment_jobsite` model begins in the Operational Analysis (OA) layer, centered on the jobsite stakeholders. The jobsite context is **highway/road construction in Europe**.

**Story being told:** a regional requirement is changing, causing a **70 mm reduction in the Motor Grader axle**. The OA model should show which stakeholders and operational activities are affected by, or drive, that change.

**Not yet specified:** which axle dimension is reduced (e.g., width/track vs. other), which regional requirement or regulation triggers it, and its effective date. These should be captured as requirements once confirmed.
